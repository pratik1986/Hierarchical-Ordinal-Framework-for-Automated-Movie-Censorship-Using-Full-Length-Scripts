File: sorted_old_to_new_filenames.txt

Description:
    Records filename corrections and movie rating updates identified during
    manual verification of the movie script dataset.

Purpose:
    While constructing the movie script corpus for the paper
    "Hierarchical Ordinal Framework for Automated Movie Censorship Using
    Full-Length Scripts" (IEEE GCAT 2025), some downloaded scripts were found
    to have incorrect filenames or outdated/inaccurate rating labels. This
    file documents the mapping from the original filenames to their corrected
    versions, ensuring consistency and traceability within the curated dataset.

Format:
    Original_Filename : Corrected_Filename

Notes:
    Entries marked "Could not determine correct filename" indicate scripts that
    required additional manual verification before inclusion in the final
    dataset.

Author:
    Pratik N. Kalamkar
===============================================================================
